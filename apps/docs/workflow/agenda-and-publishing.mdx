The agenda uses the event's configured dates, timezone, rooms, tracks, and session
formats.

## Schedule sessions

Use the agenda builder to place a session into a room and time range. Organizers
can switch between day, week, track, room, and list views while working from the
same schedule records.

The conflict engine checks:

- two sessions occupying the same room;
- a speaker scheduled in overlapping sessions;
- serial tracks containing overlapping sessions;
- sessions outside the event boundary;
- invalid or non-positive durations.

Conflicting placements are rejected or surfaced for resolution before publishing.

## Publish the agenda

Publishing exposes approved sessions with a confirmed, scheduled, or published
status through the public programme API.

Public routes include:

```text
/e/{event-slug}/sessions
/e/{event-slug}/speakers
/e/{event-slug}/agenda
/e/{event-slug}/itinerary
/e/{event-slug}/gallery
```

These pages are responsive and can be linked directly or embedded in an iframe.
They read from one public programme payload, so the schedule and speaker gallery
do not drift apart.

## Calendar updates

Schedule changes retain a stable iCalendar UID and increment the event sequence.
That lets calendar clients recognize an update instead of creating a duplicate.
