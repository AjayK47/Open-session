Evaluation plans define who reviews which submissions and how their scores are
combined.

## Create an evaluation plan

From **Evaluations**, choose the plan scope and add weighted criteria. A criterion
contains a label, guidance for the reviewer, a score range, and a weight.

Plans can be used sequentially for multiple review rounds. Reviewer progress and
aggregate results remain visible to organizers throughout a round.

## Assign reviewers

Assign one or more reviewers to each eligible submission. Reviewers see only
their assignments in the reviewer portal and can save a draft before submitting
the final review.

Reviewers may recuse themselves; organizers can reassign the proposal without
deleting the earlier assignment record.

## Optional AI assistance

When an OpenAI key and AI review are enabled, an organizer can run an assessment
against the evaluation criteria. Runs are stored separately from human reviews,
limited per submission, and can be overridden by an organizer.

<Warning>
  AI output is supporting evidence, not an automatic decision. Accept and decline
  actions remain explicit organizer operations.
</Warning>

## Accept a proposal

Acceptance connects the review workflow to delivery:

1. the submission becomes accepted;
2. a programme session is created;
3. participants are marked as speakers;
4. onboarding task assignments are generated;
5. the acceptance communication is recorded and delivered when email is enabled.

Declining a proposal records the decision without creating a session.
